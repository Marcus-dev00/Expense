Architecture

Core Capabilities

01 / DATABASE SQLITE 3.42

Embedded SQLite Engine

Records are stored exclusively in an embedded local SQLite database. Exact integer sen precision with zero remote database synchronization.

02 / CAPTURE RAM ONLY

Notification Auto-Detect

Captures payment alerts from Touch 'n Go, MAE Maybank, CIMB, Boost, and bank SMS in memory without broad SMS read permissions.

03 / SPLIT AA LEDGER

Bill Splits & Reimbursements

Advance shared expenses with fractional splits (1/2, 2/3, 3/4). Tracks receivables separately without inflating personal monthly expenditure.

04 / MERGE RON95 HOLD

Fuel Pre-Auth Auto-Merge

Pre-authorized pump holds (RON95) and refund changes are matched on the same day and consolidated into exact net spending with one tap.

05 / INBOX SNAP DECK

Drafts Review Deck

3D drum-wheel inbox for captured drafts, merchant category learning with trusted auto-approve, duplicate warnings, and cross-app transfer merge prompts.

06 / REPAIR ATOMIC RECALC

Tri-Lingual & Self-Healing

Full support for English, 简体中文, and Bahasa Malaysia. Built-in ledger calibration recalculates account balances atomically.

Setup

Enabling Auto-Detect

STEP 01 SETTINGS

Toggle in Settings

Open Mine (个人中心) in Expense and turn ON the Auto-Detect Notifications switch.

STEP 02 ACCESS

Grant Notification Access

In Android System Settings, locate Expense under Notification Access and select Allow.

STEP 03 BATTERY

Battery Optimization

Under Mine → Background Autostart Guide, set battery optimization to unrestricted for Xiaomi, Samsung, OPPO, or vivo.
